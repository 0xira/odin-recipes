Broccoli Soup

Description

This creamy broccoli soup is refreshing and packed full of important nutrients.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup raw cashews
  • 1 onion
  • 1/4 tsp ground black pepper
  • 4 cups of chopped broccoli
  • 2 large potatoes
  • 5 cups of vegetable broth

Steps

  1. Mix cashews and 1 cup vegetable broth in a blender until smooth
  2. Pour the vegetable broth into a large broth and add the potatoes
  3. Cover and cook until potatoes are tender
  4. Add cashew mixture and bring to a simmer
  5. Transfer to bowl and blend until the texture that suits you
