Brownies

Description

This brownie recipe is simple and quick to make and does not compromise on taste.

Ingredients

  • 200g plain flour
  • 150 g sugar
  • 200 g butter
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 200 g cocoa powder

Steps

  1. Pour dry ingredients into a bowl
  2. Melt the butter in the microwave
  3. Add the butter and vanilla extract to the dry mix
  4. Line a baking tray with greaseproof paper and butter
  5. Pour the mix into the baking tray and cook for 35 minutes at 180°c
