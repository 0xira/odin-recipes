Shepherd's Pie

Description

This hearty shepherd's pie using only vegan ingredients is a take on a british staple but cruelty-free.

Ingredients

  • 5 potatoes
  • 1/4 cup of soy milk
  • 1 large onion
  • 1 oz plant-based beef substitute

Steps

  1. Boil potatoes in pot for 25 minutes
  2. Stir olive oil and vegan cream cheese, and mash with the potatoes
  3. Stir fry all of your chosen vegetables such as onions
  4. Add the stir fry with gravy and add the potato layer on top
  5. Cook for 20 miutes
